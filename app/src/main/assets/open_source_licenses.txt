Online services
---------------
Only used when you turn them on and add a key: Google Gemini (AI commands,
translation, trivia, emoji ideas), KLIPY / GIPHY (GIF search, "Powered by
KLIPY / GIPHY"), claude.ai (usage widget), exchange rates for the currency chip,
website titles and icons for link previews. Their own terms apply.
